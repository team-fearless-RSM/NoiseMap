THE PROJECT

About NoiseMap

Making local sound observations easier to understand and act on.

Our approach

NoiseMap is a Team Fearless NRL Changemakers project concept. The goal is to build a system that records observations at selected locations, presents trends over time, and helps the team document awareness and follow-up actions.

Measurement integrity matters.

An LM393 sound sensor provides threshold and analogue signal information; its raw readings are not calibrated sound-pressure measurements. NoiseMap will only display dB or dB(A) if suitable calibrated measurement hardware and a documented calibration method are implemented.
