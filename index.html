THE PROJECT

About NoiseMap

Making local sound observations easier to understand and act on.

Our approach

NoiseMap is a Team Fearless NRL Changemakers project concept. The goal is to build a system that records observations at selected locations, presents trends over time, and helps the team document awareness and follow-up actions.

Measurement integrity matters.

The current LM393-based prototype detects sound activity and provides raw sensor readings. These readings represent combined sound from the surroundings; they do not identify individual sources and are not calibrated sound-pressure measurements. NoiseMap will only display dB or dB(A) if suitable calibrated measurement hardware and a documented calibration method are implemented.

ROADMAP · PLANNED RESEARCH

Future Development

Moving from measuring surrounding noise to exploring what may be causing it.

Sound-source identification

Current capability: The prototype measures combined sound activity from its surroundings. It cannot currently distinguish traffic, machinery, people talking, or other individual sources.

Future goal: AI-based sound classification

We plan to explore sound-classification technology that could analyse labelled audio samples and recognise broad categories of sound, such as traffic, machinery, or human activity. This would be a research and development step; it is not a feature of the current prototype.

Possible development pathway

1. Collect labelled samples: Build a dataset of representative audio samples from different sound categories.

2. Explore model training: Evaluate machine-learning approaches that can learn patterns from the labelled samples.

3. Integrate predictions: If testing is successful, show the model's predicted sound category alongside the measured noise observations, with clear confidence and limitations.

Responsible development

Any future audio collection would need appropriate permissions, privacy safeguards, and testing in real environments. A predicted category would be an estimate, not proof of the exact source.
